    Getting started

    Madhur Digitals keeps every school's student and staff records, their photos, and prints their ID cards.

    Logging in

    • Use the username and password you were given. The password is case-sensitive: if you see Caps Lock is on, turn it off.
    • One login works in every tab of the same browser. Opening a new tab doesn't ask again.
    • A login can be used on 2 devices at a time. On a third device you're asked whether to log out the other two. Choose Cancel if you don't recognise them, and tell your admin.
    • Log out is under your name at the top right. On a shared computer, always log out.

    Choosing a school

    Most pages work on one school. When you open one from the Dashboard or the menu, you're asked which school. If your login has only one school, you go straight to the page.

    On a phone

    Everything works on a phone. The menu is behind the ☰ button at the top left.

    Dashboard

    • The 4 numbers show students in school, students added this month (compared with last month), schools and the average per school. For one school, you see classes and the largest class instead.
    • Showing chooses all your schools or one school. You can also click a school in Students per school.
    • Students added: hover over the chart (or tap it on a phone) for exact numbers. 6 months / 12 months changes the period, and Table shows the same numbers as a table.
    • Refresh gets the latest numbers. The time next to it shows when they were last updated.
    • Quick actions and Manage show only what your login is allowed to do.

    Adding records

    Open Add records and choose the school. Use the Students or Staff tab. The boxes are the ones set up for that school.

    Typing fast

    • Enter moves to the next box (except in the address box, where it starts a new line).
    • Date of birth: type 8 digits, like 12072001. When you leave the box it becomes 12/07/2001. It must be a real date and not in the future. Students must be 1 to 80 years old, staff 12 to 100.
    • Phone, Mobile or Contact: one 10-digit mobile number starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9.
    • A mistake is shown in red under the box and stays until you fix it.
    • Name and Class are required.

    Photo

    Choose a photo file, or use Take photo to use the camera. The photo uploads after the record is saved.

    Saving

    • Press Save. The record appears in Recent entries straight away and the form clears for the next one. Class and Section stay filled in.
    • Saving finishes in the background. A row turns green when it's saved.
    • If a row says Not saved, press Retry. It's never saved twice.
    • Don't close the page while rows are still saving. The page asks you first.

    Possible duplicates

    If the record looks like someone already saved (for example the same name and date of birth), you're warned while typing. If you save anyway, the row waits under Waiting for your decision:

    • View shows both records side by side.
    • Save anyway saves it (for example, two different children with the same name).
    • Discard removes it.

    Finding and editing records

    • Search by name or Student ID, choose classes and sections, or filter by who added the record.
    • Edit changes a record and can replace its photo. The old photo is removed only after the new one is saved.
    • When editing, the date of birth and phone rules are checked only for what you change, so an old record can always be edited.
    • Left marks a student who has left the school. Re-admit brings them back with the same Student ID; you choose the new class.
    • Delete asks first. Deleted records can be brought back with Restore deleted (if your login has it).
    • 📅 Session: earlier sessions can be opened to look at and reprint, but not changed.

    Photos

    • Open Photos and choose the school to see every student with or without a photo.
    • Drag many photo files at once. Each file is matched to a student, and you decide before anything is replaced.
    • Click a student's photo to see it larger, or to add or replace just that one.

    Bulk upload (Excel)

    1. Download the sample file to see the layout, or use your own Excel sheet.
    2. Load the file. Columns are matched to the school's boxes automatically; check them and correct any that are wrong.
    3. Rows with problems are highlighted with the reason (for example a date of birth that isn't a real date, or a phone that isn't one 10-digit mobile number), and possible duplicates are marked. Fix cells right there, or download the problem rows as Excel.
    4. Phone numbers that only need tidying (spaces, dashes, +91 or a leading 0) can be fixed together: press Review and fix, check the list, untick any you don't want, then Fix ticked. Fixed cells turn green, and Undo fix puts them back. Nothing is saved until Submit selected. Two numbers in one cell must be fixed by hand.
    5. Tick the rows to upload and press Submit selected. A batch is never uploaded twice, even if you press it again.

    Printing ID cards

    • Open Print cards, choose the school, then the classes and sections. You can search for a student or select everyone.
    • Generate cards shows the cards. Then use Print, or Download PDF.
    • The back sheet and the verification checklist (for the school to check every detail) are on the same page.
    • Cards are printed for the school's current session. Earlier sessions can only be reprinted by an admin.
    • Every print is recorded.

    Export

    • Choose the fields, classes and sections, then download Excel or the cards as PDF.
    • Every export is recorded before the file is made.

    School forms

    Print blank forms for a school to fill in by hand, for students or staff. Staff and vendor logins can also make a form for a new school that isn't in the system yet (Other school).

    New session and promotion

    • A school's session usually starts in March (for example 2026-27). It can be set per school.
    • New session promotes classes for the new year: every class line can be checked and changed before you confirm.
    • Undo is possible until the first change in the new session (any edit, add, delete or print).
    • Session pipeline shows where every school stands: Due → Promoted → Checklist printed → Cards printed.

    Schools

    Schools lists your schools. There you change a school's details, the boxes on its form, its card template and its session.

    Card templates

    Card templates designs how a school's ID card looks. Choose a school, or no school to work on the shared library.

    Restore deleted

    Deleted records stay here and can be brought back to their school. Deleting from here is permanent, so it asks first.

    Users and permissions

    • Add a login, choose its role and schools, and tick what it may do (add, manage, print, export and so on). People see only the pages and buttons they're allowed.
    • Reset a password or archive a login that's no longer needed.

    Vendors

    Partners (vendors) and their branding: name, logo and colour. A vendor's people see the vendor's look, and their own login link.

    Common problems

    "You don't have permission to access …"
    Your login isn't allowed to open that page. Ask your admin if you need it.
    "Already logged in elsewhere"
    This login is open on 2 other devices. Log out there, or choose to log them out here.
    A row says "Not saved"
    The internet dropped. Press Retry; nothing is saved twice.
    The date of birth turned red
    Type exactly 8 digits: day, month, year, like 05012015. Check the year too: students must be 1 to 80 years old, staff 12 to 100.
    "A mobile number starts with 6, 7, 8 or 9"
    Only Indian mobile numbers are accepted, one per box, without +91 or 0 in front.
    Charts don't show on the Dashboard
    They need an internet connection. The numbers are still shown.
    Numbers look old
    Press Refresh on the Dashboard.
